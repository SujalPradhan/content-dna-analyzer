How it works
1 · MeasureShot cuts, one frame per shot, motion and loudness are measured on our server. Whisper writes a word-timed transcript; Gemini describes what is on screen.
2 · CompareAbout 20 measurements are checked against researched guidance and against the typical range for the genre in a corpus of real YouTube videos.
3 · FixClaude writes timestamped fixes that must cite the measurements. A checker verifies every cited number, quote and frame before it is shown.

Fixes are suggestions to test, not predictions of views. Results for these videos are pre-computed, so this page needs no server. Analyze your own video · Cost and time per video
