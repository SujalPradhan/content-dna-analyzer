Methods

Shots: PySceneDetect AdaptiveDetector. Motion and colour: OpenCV at 2 fps. Loudness: librosa RMS. Transcript: whisper-1 word timestamps, screened for invented passages. Screen formats and calls to action: gemini-3.5-flash on the YouTube URL. Critique: claude-sonnet-5 with structured output; every cited metric, quote and visual is re-checked by a deterministic validator. Judge: claude-opus-4-8, blind. Statistics: Kruskal-Wallis with Benjamini-Hochberg, Spearman with bootstrap intervals and within-genre permutation tests. Regenerate everything with analysis/*.py.
